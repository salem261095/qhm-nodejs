About QHM
Saudi Corporate Law Firm.

Qaisar Hamed Metawea Law Firm (QHM) is a Saudi corporate law firm headquartered in Jeddah with a strong presence in Riyadh, advising multinational companies and large enterprises across critical sectors.

We operate with a partner-led model, ensuring senior oversight and regulatory certainty across all mandates.
Strategic Advisory Focus

Our practice is built on strategic advisory rather than reactive legal support. We guide corporates through Saudi Arabia’s evolving legal framework, focusing on:
Market Entry & FDI

Structuring foreign investment and regional headquarters setups.
Corporate Governance

Navigating regulatory compliance and protecting enterprise value.
Tax & Zakat

ZATCA advisory, dispute representation, and transfer pricing.
Cross-Border Transactions

Executing complex M&A, joint ventures, and international commercial agreements.
Government Engagements

Advising on ministries, public institutions, and regulatory changes.
Commercial Disputes

High-value litigation strategy and arbitration support.
