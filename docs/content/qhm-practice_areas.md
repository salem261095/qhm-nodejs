## Tax
The Firm has distinguished experience providing legal solutions in the dynamic tax environment in Saudi Arabia. The Firm is proud to be one of the first law firms to represent Clients before the newly established Committees for the Resolution of Tax Violations and Disputes, under the aegis of the General Secretariat of Tax Committees. We also work closely with tax consultants to advise Clients on compliance with the emerging tax regulations, to enable our Clients to meet both the needs of the business and legal requirements.
Our services in relation to tax law include:
- Advising on compliance and regulatory issues
- Providing structuring solutions
- Providing extensive legal opinions regarding the scope and application of the Saudi tax laws and regulations
- Representing Clients before the Internal Committee at the General Authority for Zakat and Tax
- Representing Clients before the Committees for Resolution of Tax Violations and Disputes
